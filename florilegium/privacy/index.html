Florilegium

Privacy policy

Draft 2026-10-30 · Not yet in effect

This policy describes how Elsewhere Index LLC (“Elsewhere Index”, “the team”) handles information in Florilegium, a flower-education app published by Elsewhere Index LLC and made by Twelve Thirty Four. Contact: florilegium@twelvethirtyfourflowers.com.

1. Summary

  • Florilegium has no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Florilegium has no ads and no advertising SDKs. It does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt. It does not track you.
  • Your progress, quiz results, Collection and settings are stored on your device, in a local database. The team does not receive them.
  • Purchases are billed by Apple or Google. RevenueCat manages entitlements.
  • Two optional data streams exist: anonymous usage data (TelemetryDeck) and crash reports (Sentry). You can turn each off in Settings (section 4).

2. What stays on your device

The following is stored locally and is not sent to the team or to any third party: lesson progress, review schedule, weekly visit counts, quiz answers and results, the pressed-flower Collection, reminder times, and app settings. Deleting the app removes it. Settings includes a control to reset progress.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They use no push service and no server.

If you share a quiz result card, the app creates the image on your device and hands it to the system share sheet. The app does not upload it.

3. Third parties

Florilegium uses five third parties. Each receives only the data listed here.

No screenshots, no session replay and no performance tracing are collected. Session data carries one random per-install ID that the Sentry SDK generates on the device. It is used only to count sessions and crashes, is never linked to a person, and is not the advertising ID or the vendor ID.
TelemetryDeckAnonymous usage analytics (only while Share anonymous usage data is on)Events such as lesson started, lesson completed and paywall viewed, each with parameters from a closed list (for example a lesson number or a bucketed accuracy range). The app sends no name, email, free text or advertising identifier. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses.

The team does not sell personal data and does not share it for advertising. No other third party receives data from the app. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Where the app is offered, and your choices

Florilegium is offered in the United States. Anonymous usage data and crash reports are on when you first open the app. The first-run screen says so, and you can turn either off there or later in Settings.

Turning a switch off takes effect immediately. While usage data is off, nothing is sent to TelemetryDeck or queued for later. While crash reports are off, Sentry does not start and errors are discarded. Debug builds of the app send nothing.

5. Controller and your rights

Elsewhere Index LLC is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Usage data and crash reports rest on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the Settings switches. To ask about data, email florilegium@twelvethirtyfourflowers.com.

6. What the team can and cannot look up

Because the app has no accounts and the team holds no identifier that links to you, the team may be unable to find data about a particular person. Data on your device is under your control. Data held by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Sentry or TelemetryDeck is covered by their processes. Questions about data requests: florilegium@twelvethirtyfourflowers.com.

Retention

Sentry keeps error and event data for 30 days. For TelemetryDeck, see its privacy policy.

Links to other businesses

Links to Twelve Thirty Four and other businesses open their own app or site. Florilegium sends no personal data with them.

7. Children

Florilegium is a general-audience education app and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information, so it holds no information that identifies a child.

8. Security

Traffic from the app to RevenueCat, Sentry and TelemetryDeck is encrypted in transit. The app stores no passwords or payment details.

9. International transfers

Providers may process data in the United States and other countries under their own terms.

10. Changes

When this policy changes, the “Last updated” date changes. If the app begins to collect a new kind of data, the in-app privacy information and the App Store privacy labels change in the same release.
