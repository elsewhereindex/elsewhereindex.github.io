Support

Draft 2026-10-28

Contact

Email florilegium@twelvethirtyfourflowers.com. Include your device model, operating system version and the app version (Settings, About). Do not send payment card details.

Purchases and subscriptions

  • Restore purchases: open the paywall or Settings and choose Restore purchases.
  • Cancel an Apple subscription: Settings on your iPhone, your name, Subscriptions.
  • Cancel a Google Play subscription: Play Store, profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or from Google Play. The team cannot issue refunds.

Your data

Progress is stored on your device. To remove it, use Settings, Reset progress, or delete the app. Usage data and crash reports are optional and have switches in Settings. Details are in the privacy policy. Because the app has no accounts, there is no account to delete.

Report a content error

Email the entry name, the text in question and, if you have one, a source. The team checks citations before changing an entry.

Terms

See the terms of use, which include the Apple standard EULA.
