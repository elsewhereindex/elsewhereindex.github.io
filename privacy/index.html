Privacy policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Elsewhere Index LLC is a small software studio in Dallas. We make consumer apps, including Dear and Whim. Each app has its own privacy policy for the people who use it: Dear and Whim. This page covers the company itself and the tools we use to run it, including how we use Pinterest.

Our Pinterest publishing tool

We run a publishing tool, Elsewhere Index Publisher, that connects to our own Pinterest business accounts for Dear and Whim. It connects through Pinterest's standard OAuth sign-in. We never ask for or store a Pinterest password. With the access Pinterest grants, the tool:

  • creates Pins and boards on our own accounts, using images, titles, descriptions and links that we made and approved;
  • reads basic information about our own accounts, boards and Pins;
  • reads the totals Pinterest reports for our own Pins, such as impressions, saves and clicks, so we can see which Pins are useful.

The tool does not connect anyone else's Pinterest account. It does not scrape Pinterest, and it does not collect information about the people who see, save or click our Pins beyond the aggregate totals Pinterest reports to us.

How we store it

The access token is stored in the encrypted keychain of a company computer and is used only to post and read our own Pins. We do not share, sell or rent any data we get from Pinterest. Pin totals are kept as simple daily counts. If we stop using the tool, we delete the token and revoke its access.

Other company information

If you email us or use the contact form on this site, we keep your message and address to reply to you and do not use them for anything else. This website does not use advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. We will reply within 30 days.
